## English (en-US), default

**App name (30):**
Paskle: CNA, HHA & Permit Prep

**Short description (80):**
Practice CNA, HHA and Massachusetts permit tests in Kreyòl, French or Spanish.

**Full description:**

Paskle helps you pass your exam in the language you're most comfortable with. Every question and lesson is in Haitian Creole, French or Spanish, with the English right beside it, so you learn the words you will see on the real test.

Kreyòl: Pratike pou egzamen CNA, HHA ak pèmi kondui Massachusetts an Kreyòl, ak Anglè bò kote.

WHAT YOU CAN STUDY
• Massachusetts learner's permit: 181 questions in 8 chapters, road signs, and a practice test with the same length and passing score as the RMV test (25 questions, 18 to pass).
• Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA): questions follow the Massachusetts written exam outline, with a 60-question timed practice exam.
• Home Health Aide (HHA): all 14 subjects in federal home health aide training.

HOW IT HELPS
• Study guides written in plain language, chapter by chapter.
• Every answer explains why, and shows the source and page it comes from.
• Timed practice exams and an estimated score, so you know when you're ready.
• Review the questions you missed.
• Key English terms with their meaning in your language.
• A 62-question FAQ on getting your Massachusetts permit and license.
• Listen to questions read aloud in English.
• Works offline after the first visit.

LANGUAGES
Kreyòl + English · Français + English · Español + English · English

Paskle is an independent study tool. It is not affiliated with the Massachusetts RMV, the Department of Public Health or any testing company. Content is based on free public sources, such as the Massachusetts Driver's Manual and open nursing assistant textbooks, and each source is cited in the app. Always check the latest official manual.
