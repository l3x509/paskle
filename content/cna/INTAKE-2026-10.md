# CNA question intake: October 2026 batch (24 candidates)

Origin: an outside AI draft of 52 CNA questions. 28 were dropped as duplicates of the bank or of each other (see the end of this file).
The 24 below were matched to free sources on 2026-10-18. No question goes into `cna_src.py` until:

- its fact is stated in a guide section (added or existing);
- that section cites the source;
- weak distractors are rewritten so the wrong answers are plausible.

Legend:

- **OK**: the source states the answer; link and write it.
- **Rewrite**: the source supports a corrected version (change shown).
- **New bullet**: the source is found, but the guide has no bullet yet; add it first.
- **Hold**: no free source found yet, or it needs RN sign-off.
