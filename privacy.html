Paskle Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026 · Lexcellent Solutions LLC

EnglishKreyòlTerms of useBack to Paskle

Paskle helps you practice for the CNA, home health aide and Massachusetts learner's permit exams. This policy covers the Paskle website (paskleapp.com) and the Paskle Android app.

What we collect

Paskle does not collect, send or sell your personal information. You do not need an account to use it.

  • Your answers, scores, language, chosen exam, exam date and any name or email you type in the app are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Paskle has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • The "Listen" button uses your device's built-in text-to-speech.

Website hosting

When you open paskleapp.com, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site running and secure. We do not use these logs to identify you.

Links to other sites

Lessons link to the public sources they are based on, such as Mass.gov and the open nursing assistant textbook. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Because your progress is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app (on the web: clear this site's data in your browser).

Children

Paskle is meant for people preparing for licensing and certification exams, who are generally 16 or older. It is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If Paskle starts collecting any information, for example to support accounts or purchases, we will update this policy before that change goes live.

Contact

Questions: privacy@paskleapp.com

Politik sou vi prive (Kreyòl)

Paskle ede w pratike pou egzamen CNA, ed swen a domisil (HHA) ak pèmi apranti Massachusetts. Politik sa a kouvri sit entènèt Paskle (paskleapp.com) ak aplikasyon Android Paskle.

Kisa nou kolekte

Paskle pa kolekte, pa voye epi pa vann enfòmasyon pèsonèl ou. Ou pa bezwen yon kont pou w sèvi avè l.

  • Repons ou, nòt ou, lang ou, egzamen ou chwazi, dat egzamen w ak nenpòt non oswa imèl ou tape nan aplikasyon an rete sèlman sou aparèy ou. Yo pa voye yo ba nou ni ba pèsonn.
  • Paskle pa gen piblisite epi li pa gen zouti estatistik oswa swivi.
  • Bouton "Koute" a sèvi ak fonksyon lekti vwa ki deja nan aparèy ou.

Sit entènèt la

Lè w louvri paskleapp.com, konpayi ki estoke sit la ka kenbe jounal sèvè nòmal, tankou adrès IP ou, kalite navigatè w ak paj ou mande yo, pou sit la mache byen e an sekirite. Nou pa sèvi ak jounal sa yo pou idantifye w.

Efase done ou

Paske pwogrè w rete sèlman sou aparèy ou, ou ka efase l nenpòt lè lè w efase done aplikasyon an oswa lè w dezenstale l (sou entènèt: efase done sit la nan navigatè w).

Timoun

Paskle fèt pou moun k ap prepare egzamen lisans ak sètifikasyon, ki gen jeneralman 16 an oswa plis. Li pa fèt pou timoun ki poko gen 13 an.
